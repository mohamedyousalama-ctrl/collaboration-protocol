# Limitations and Evidence Status

## Current status
The preserved historical artifact is a 52-record **candidate naturalistic corpus**. It is not yet defensible to describe all 52 records as fully transcript-verified and researcher-final classified.

## Known limitations
1. **Single-researcher/self-data context.** The corpus originates from one researcher's own AI work conversations. It can establish mechanisms and generate hypotheses, but it does not by itself estimate population prevalence.
2. **LLM-assisted extraction.** Candidate incidents were extracted with an LLM under a fixed forensic protocol. Extraction is not treated as ground-truth classification.
3. **Researcher-final coding incomplete.** Historical researcher-only classification fields were not completed across all 52 records in the preserved version.
4. **Primary transcript availability incomplete.** The archive states that the AUD raw transcript was recovered for verbatim checking, but current repository discovery has not yet established independently readable primary transcripts for every one of the seven source families.
5. **Competence-versus-intent contamination risk.** Some extracted candidates may represent factual/technical competence errors or premise errors rather than an interpretation-of-intent event. These require filtering before any SII rate is calculated.
6. **Selection effects.** The corpus was collected from complex AI-assisted work where governance/rejection mechanisms often exposed errors quickly. Detection latency and observed consequence rates may therefore not generalize to ordinary users.
7. **No forced N=52.** Validation may reduce the final analyzable set. Exclusion and unverified status are legitimate outcomes.
8. **No causal bridge to DCM 2.0 is assumed.** Any link between upstream interpretation mechanics and downstream responsibility attribution must be established by schema comparison and future study design, not inferred from conceptual similarity.

## Evidence language currently allowed
Appropriate:
- "52 extracted candidate incidents"
- "documented LLM-assisted extraction protocol"
- "researcher-only classification framework"
- "validation in progress"

Not yet appropriate:
- "52 validated Silent Intent Inference events"
- "all 52 incidents are transcript-confirmed"
- "the corpus proves prevalence"
- "CP and DCM 2.0 already form one connected dataset"
