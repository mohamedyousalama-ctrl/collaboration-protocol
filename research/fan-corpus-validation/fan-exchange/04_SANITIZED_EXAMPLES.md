## Inclusion rule
An example may be added only after:
1. the incident survives structural audit;
2. its source transcript is located or the example is explicitly labeled unverified;
3. quoted text is checked where possible;
4. sensitive project/person information is removed or generalized without changing the research-relevant structure;
5. the example's classification is clearly labeled as either assistant recommendation or researcher-final decision.
