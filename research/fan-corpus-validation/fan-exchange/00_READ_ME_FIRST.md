## Exchange principle

The package keeps five epistemic layers separate:

- **observable interaction evidence** — what the underlying source actually records;
- **LLM-assisted extraction** — candidate A-fields produced under the extraction protocol;
- **assistant audit/recommendation** — later quality-control and proposed classifications;
- **researcher adjudication** — the authoritative C-fields, especially C5 true intent;
- **cross-study interpretation** — any connection to DCM 2.0, which requires agreement by both researchers.

No incomplete field is silently promoted to validated evidence, and no unavailable primary transcript is treated as if it had been checked.

## What Fan is being asked to evaluate

At this stage Fan does not need to accept CP's hypotheses or the 52 records as validated incidents. The useful question is narrower:

> **Does this extraction/classification structure contain variables that can be meaningfully compared with the DCM 2.0 field-study variables, and if so, where are the compatible and incompatible levels of observation?**

That is the purpose of this package.
