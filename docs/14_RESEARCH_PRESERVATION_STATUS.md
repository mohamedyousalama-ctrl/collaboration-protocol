## Preservation objective

Preserve the recoverable Collaboration Protocol research program in a form that remains academically legible even when historical files use conflicting terminology, dates, empirical denominators, or version labels.

The archive therefore separates:

- frozen CP v1.0.1 semantics;
- earlier SII / IPP / ICD / IFI / Context Roots theory;
- empirical and naturalistic-corpus materials;
- Klear implementation/evaluation evidence;
- Safety Floor and domain extensions;
- Ghost/Continuity and applied governance derivatives;
- current authorization-continuity research;
- provenance, publication, and IP records;
- contradictions and unresolved questions.

## Integrity rules

1. Original recovered text is not silently rewritten to make versions agree.
2. Unsupported 2025 priority claims are not promoted; the recovered provenance supports CP activity from January 2026.
3. Historical hypotheses, simulations, and incomplete measurements remain labeled as such.
4. Frozen CP v1.0.1 is not silently extended by later research.
5. Credential-bearing material is excluded; its exclusion is recorded by hash.
6. Third-party template material is excluded where it is not original CP research.
7. SHA-256 manifests are maintained for the preserved staging set and recovered bundles.

## Completion criterion

The preservation pass is complete only when every substantively CP-related artifact recovered from the available research library/source bundles is either:

- committed directly in searchable form;
- preserved through a lossless archival representation;
- represented by a content-preserving text/tabular conversion plus source hash; or
- explicitly listed as excluded, unavailable, duplicate, or third-party material.

A final audit must compare the repository tree against the asset register and manifests before the preservation branch is merged to `main`.
