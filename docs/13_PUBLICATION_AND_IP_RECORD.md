## 8. Novelty and ownership wording

Safe academic wording in this repository is:

- “introduced in the CP research program”;
- “named/formalized by Mohamed Salama in the recovered CP artifacts”;
- “proposed construct”; or
- “working hypothesis.”

Avoid stating “first ever,” “unique worldwide,” or “patented” without the relevant prior-art/legal evidence.
