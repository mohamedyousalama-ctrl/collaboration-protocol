# Appendix I: Intent Decay Mathematical Model — Deferred

## I.1 Concept Summary

**Intent decay** refers to cumulative divergence between original intent and system output caused by compounded assumptions. A mathematical model would formalize how decay propagates.

## I.2 Modeling Intuition
- Each silent inference introduces deviation δ
- Deviation compounds across n steps
- Total decay D = f(δ₁, δ₂, ... δₙ)
- CP reduces decay by minimizing silent inferences at critical pivots

## I.3 Visualization Approach

This work **visualizes** decay conceptually rather than claiming a finalized mathematical model. Formal modeling requires empirical data.

## I.4 Research Questions (Deferred)

## I.5 Why Deferred

# Appendix J: Legal Framework: Meaning Ownership — Deferred

## J.1 Concept Summary

**Meaning ownership** refers to the legal right of users to define acceptable interpretations of their expressed intent. This establishes a legal foundation for the Collaboration Protocol's core principle.

## J.2 Legal Alignment

Meaning ownership aligns with:
- **Existing consent principles:** Users must consent to how their data/intent is processed
- **Explainability requirements:** GDPR Art. 22 requires explanation of automated decisions
- **Accountability law trajectories:** Emerging AI liability frameworks emphasize process accountability

## J.3 Proposed Legal Primitives

| Primitive | Definition | Legal Basis |
|-----------|------------|-------------|

| **Right to Pivot Notification** | Users must be informed of semantic branching points | Extension of explainability |
| **Right to Override** | Users may redirect AI at any point | Extension of consent withdrawal |
| **Right to Audit** | Users may inspect how their intent was transformed | Extension of access rights |

- Legal scholarship integration (law faculty collaboration)
