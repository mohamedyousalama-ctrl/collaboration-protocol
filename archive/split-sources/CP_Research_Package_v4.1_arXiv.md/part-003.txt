## 6.3 CP Compliance Criteria

A system is **CP-compliant** if and only if it:
- Exposes Intent Pivot Points according to the user's ICD setting
- Respects user choices at all surfaced pivot points
- Allows human override at any stage of generation
- Prevents silent resolution of intent-critical ambiguity
- Maintains traceable logs of all intent transformations

# 7. Proposed Empirical Validation

The following studies are designed to validate the Collaboration Protocol's core claims. Each study is specified with sufficient detail for independent replication.

## 7.1 Study 1: Silent Inference Prevalence

### Research Question
How frequently do current AI systems engage in Silent Intent Inference, and how often are users aware of these inferences?

### Hypotheses
- **H1a:** Silent intent inferences occur in >60% of complex task completions
- **H1b:** Users are unaware of these inferences in >70% of cases

### Method
- **Participants:** 200 knowledge workers recruited via Prolific
- **Task:** Complete 5 complex tasks using ChatGPT or Claude (randomized)
- **Tasks:** Recommendation letter, project proposal, data analysis request, creative brief, technical explanation
- **Data:** Full interaction transcripts saved
- **Coding:** 4 trained annotators identify all assumption points using IPP codebook
- **Awareness measure:** Post-task survey asks "What decisions did the AI make for you?" (open-ended)
- **Comparison:** Match user-reported assumptions against coder-identified assumptions

### Analysis
- **Silent Inference Rate** = (Total coded assumptions) / (Total interactions)
- **Awareness Rate** = (User-identified assumptions) / (Coder-identified assumptions)
- **Inter-rater reliability:** Cohen's κ computed for assumption coding

### Expected Output
Quantified baseline of silent inference prevalence in current AI systems, establishing the magnitude of the problem CP addresses.

## 7.2 Study 2: IPP Annotation Reliability

### Research Question
Can Intent Pivot Points be reliably identified by trained annotators?

### Method
- **Corpus:** 100 human-AI interaction transcripts from Study 1
- **Annotators:** 4 trained coders + 2 expert validators
- **Training:** 8-hour training session using IPP codebook
- **Task:** Independently annotate all IPPs in each transcript
- **Measure:** Cohen's κ for pairwise agreement; Fleiss' κ for group agreement

### Success Criteria
- κ > 0.7 indicates acceptable reliability
- κ > 0.8 indicates good reliability

If reliability is achieved, IPP becomes a validated construct for future research.

## 7.3 Study 3: ICD Effect on Collaboration Quality

### Research Question
Does Intent Control Degree affect intent fidelity and user satisfaction?

### Hypotheses
- **H3a:** Higher ICD leads to higher Intent Fidelity Index scores
- **H3b:** The ICD-satisfaction relationship is moderated by task complexity
- **H3c:** Higher ICD increases task completion time but reduces revision cycles

### Design
4 (ICD: 0.2, 0.5, 0.8, 1.0) × 3 (Complexity: Low, Medium, High) mixed design
- ICD: Between-subjects
- Complexity: Within-subjects

### Method
- **Participants:** 240 (60 per ICD condition)
- **Interface:** Custom AI wrapper implementing ICD conditions
- **Tasks:** 3 tasks of varying complexity (document draft, analysis, creative work)
- **Measures:** IFI (composite), task time, revision count, NASA-TLX, satisfaction

### Analysis
Two-way mixed ANOVA; planned contrasts for ICD main effect; moderation analysis for complexity interaction.

## 7.4 Study 4: IPP Intervention Effect

### Research Question
Does user intervention at Intent Pivot Points improve output alignment?

### Design
4 conditions (between-subjects):
1. **Control:** Standard generation, no pivot exposure
2. **Post-hoc:** Pivots shown after generation
3. **Real-time:** Pivots shown during generation
4. **Interactive:** User can edit pivots mid-generation

### Method
- **Participants:** 160 (40 per condition)
- **Task:** Complex document with multiple valid interpretations
- **Measures:** Output alignment (expert-rated), revision cycles, perceived control, trust

### Predictions
Interactive > Real-time > Post-hoc > Control on alignment measures.

# 9. Limitations and Scope

## 9.1 Explicit Scope Boundaries

This research program is explicitly bounded:
- No live production system deployment in initial phases
- No formal mathematical optimization proofs
- No cross-cultural validation (deferred to Phase 4)
- No claims about model architecture modifications

These boundaries are methodological choices, not oversights.

## 9.2 Potential Limitations

- **Ecological validity:** Lab tasks may not fully represent real-world complexity
- **Participant pool:** Prolific samples may not generalize to all user populations
- **IPP subjectivity:** Despite reliability testing, edge cases may remain ambiguous
- **ICD manipulation:** Interface-based ICD may not capture all dimensions of control

## 9.3 What This Research Does NOT Address

- Model training modifications
- Architectural changes to LLMs
- Fully autonomous AI systems
- Non-language modalities (vision, robotics)
