ble refinement of the ICD model.
- **Surfaced-IPP decay:** an explicitly surfaced ambiguity that receives no human answer reverts to silent resolution in the next occurrence (AUD-015 vs the SCOPE012 surfacing). Implies a protocol requirement: *unresolved surfaced IPPs must persist, not expire.*
- **Post-correction adaptation:** after one harsh correction (AUD-005), the same model began proactively surfacing ambiguities (AUD-022) with zero subsequent rework — the micro-timescale mirror of Fan's experience gradient.
- **Content-as-command (authority IPP, maximal materiality):** pasting executable content with no imperative was treated as authorization to execute against production (W39-007). Converges with Klear's credential-challenge findings from the controlled side.
- **Means-vs-outcome fixation:** model silently binds an outcome instruction ("one commit, one draft PR") to a particular means (GitHub CLI auth) and stalls ~5 exchanges — the corpus's longest awareness gap, showing detection latency varies by failure type (KBD-004).

# PART IV — WHAT IS ESTABLISHED SCIENCE vs INVENTED vs HYPOTHESIZED

## Layer 1 — Established science (the foundation, not Mohamed's)
Five research streams: LLM uncertainty quantification (Xiong et al. 2024; Ghasemabadi & Niu 2025 — cited for IPP-detection feasibility); human-in-the-loop and mixed-initiative interaction; AI transparency/explainability; accountability research in HCI; linguistic ambiguity research. Also: "trained annotators" is standard method, not an invention — Mohamed is first to apply it to IPPs.

## Layer 2 — Invented (all constructs in Part III are Mohamed's originals)
The problem naming (SII), IPP, ICD, IFI, CR, SMP, CF, detection algorithm, IPP logging, Seven Laws + Law 8, SF/SCS/IFI-H, Intent Signatures, and the corpus-derived constructs (standing-instruction IPPs, resolution-by-disclosure, surfaced-IPP decay).

## Layer 3 — Hypothesized (falsifiable, not yet proven)
- **H1 (Prevalence):** unstated intent assumptions in >60% of complex interactions; users unaware in >70% of cases.
- **H2 (Awareness Gap):** users are usually unaware when SII occurs. *Mechanism candidate from Fan's FN-006: operational confidence conceals conceptual gaps.*
- **H3 (ICD Effect):** higher ICD → higher fidelity/satisfaction at time cost, with task-dependent optimum (experimental design: ICD 0.2/0.5/0.8/1.0 × task complexity; 200+ knowledge workers).
- **H4 (IPP Reliability):** trained annotators identify IPPs at κ > 0.7; intervention at IPPs predicts satisfaction.
- **H5 (Safety-Fidelity Trade-off):** H5a fidelity and clinical safety diverge in health; H5b IFI-H reduces but doesn't eliminate the gap; H5c SF achieves SCS ≥ 0.90 without reducing satisfaction.
- Tier-2/3 claims: meaning-ownership erosion; articulation assistance without usurpation (suggestions > auto-resolution > no help); Intent Signature portability (~30% faster); CP reduces high-stakes errors; reversibility calibrates trust.
- Simulations (κ = 0.61–0.81; 22–489 ms) show the framework is *computable*, not *true* — a restraint the paper states explicitly and that Fan independently praised.

**Headline patterns:** the stale-base standing-instruction cluster (3 independent windows; user-quoted anchor: "Four stale-base incidents on this project would each have deleted a day of merged work" → first defensible cost figure ≈ 4 workdays); ~40% benign outcomes (20/52 suggested) — the selection-bias defense and the argument for materiality-scaled rather than universal surfacing; detection latency almost uniformly 1 turn **because** the KIVO rejection-gate governance catches mismatches immediately → the corpus measures SII under *near-optimal oversight*, so all rates are **lower bounds**; and the meta-replication — two different models (AUD-018, CDX-005) each logged performing the extraction as itself a silent inference.

**Critical methods rule discovered during triage:** the extraction format dresses **competence errors** (false beliefs about facts/tech, e.g. SQL syntax) as "interpretation choices." Hence field **C0 INCIDENT CLASS** (INTENT / COMPETENCE / PREMISE / MIXED / META) — only INTENT feeds the SII rate; the competence set becomes a finding in itself. Classification and quote-verification (C9) are in progress and are prerequisites for any published number.
