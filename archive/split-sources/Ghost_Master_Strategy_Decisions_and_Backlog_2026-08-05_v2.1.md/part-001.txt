 work.

Working competitive feeling:

# 3. FOUNDER DECISION REGISTER

## FD-01 - Product essence

**Decision:** Staged combination.

Ghost combines continuity, governed delegation, Presence, protection, action, and long-term ambient intelligence in stages. The product must not reduce itself to only memory, only guardrails, only a companion, only an agent, or only a protocol.

**Implication:** One capability must lead each version commercially, while the other layers support the experience and long-term thesis.

## FD-02 - Buyer and value direction

**Founder direction:** Ghost is for people who need to save time and money using AI and achieve materially higher results than their peers.

**Refined behavioral first-buyer thesis:**

An AI-intensive operator whose attention has become the bottleneck, including founders, consultants, agency owners, engineering leads, researchers, product leaders, operations leaders, and executives who:

- use several AI systems;
- manage long-running consequential work;
- repeatedly reconstruct context;
- personally review outputs because mistakes matter;
- pay for multiple tools already;
- lose time through rework, wrong scope, repeated corrections, and forgotten rules;
- want to delegate more safely.

**Still open:** First profession, first recurring costly event, first connector, and willingness-to-pay proof.

## FD-03 - Ghost action ability

**Founder decision in principle:**

- Connected tools execute under Ghost's direction.
- Ghost may perform simple native actions when needed to prevent waste or damage.
- Ghost must retain a useful minimum action path in any app or environment.
- Ghost must not remain only a monitor or alert system.

**For-you resolution:** Adopt a Universal Action Grammar and three action domains.

### Domain 1 - Ghost-native actions

Ghost can always, within its own controlled boundary:

- preserve;
- prepare;
- route;
- request;
- resume;
- protect;
- verify;
- prove.

### Domain 2 - Connected simple actions

Where supported by an operating system, application intent, MCP tool, API, OAuth scope, or approved connector, Ghost may perform bounded actions such as:

- create a draft;
- create a task or reminder;
- save a non-destructive copy;
- organize or label approved material;
- open or focus a destination;
- queue without sending;
- create tentative personal blocks;
- pause Ghost-managed automation;
- restore Ghost-owned state;
- revoke a pending Ghost-issued permit.

### Domain 3 - Consequential external actions

Actions such as sending, publishing, buying, paying, deleting, overwriting, changing permissions, committing, pushing, merging, deploying, disclosing sensitive information, or affecting another person's rights require an Execution Permit or a narrow approved standing rule.

### Automatic-action rule

Ghost may act without asking at that moment only when all are true:

- active confirmed Context;
- explicit standing authority for the action class;
- low materiality;
- reversible;
- no new external commitment;
- no meaningful third-party effect;
- no new data access;
- bounded time and resource cost;
- outcome can be verified;
- recovery path exists.

## FD-04 - Rightness and intervention control

**Founder direction:** Use the research inventions and methodologies to create a modifiable controller that changes how Ghost acts according to confidence, risk, context, and other relevant factors.

**For-you resolution:** Create a Ghost Intervention and Authority Controller with two non-collapsible layers.

### Layer A - Deterministic hard floors

These cannot be averaged away by a favorable score:

- no valid authority;
- self-expansion of permissions;
- unapproved sensitive-data disclosure;
- consent on behalf of another person;
- Safety Floor violation;
- irreversible out-of-scope action;
- unapproved new sensor, account, data destination, or executor;
- claimed gating where Ghost technically cannot gate;
- prohibited child, bystander, or workplace surveillance.

### Layer B - Adaptive semantic intervention

After hard floors pass, evaluate:

- interpretation uncertainty;
- Context confidence;
- authority confidence;
- semantic materiality;
- severity;
- reversibility;
- third-party impact;
- data sensitivity;
- scope deviation;
- resource exposure;
- urgency;
- user control profile;
- actual connector capability;
- applicable Safety Floor.

Possible outputs:

- record;
- whisper;
- disclose and proceed;
- disclose and wait;
- discuss;
- request approval;
- hold;
- stop;
- recover;
- escalate.

User-facing modes may include Quiet, Advisor, Gatekeeper, Protected, and Custom, while the internal controller remains multidimensional.

## FD-05 - CP and Ghost relationship

**Decision in principle:** Ghost is the sole customer-facing product. CP is a protected internal governance kernel and intellectual source inside Ghost.

### Product consequence

Customers experience one Ghost product, not multiple protocols.

Internally:

- frozen CP v1.0.1 remains historically intact;
- Ghost uses CP-derived governance for material ambiguity, verified intent, human ownership of ambiguity, friction, reversibility, and accountability;
- long-term continuity, Presence, learning, action, supervision, recovery, and ambient intelligence remain Ghost product systems;
- product implementation versions do not silently rewrite research versions;
- product claims must identify which subsystem provides which guarantee.

### Canonical rule

> One product outside; separated responsibilities inside.

## FD-06 - Family and enterprise supervision

**Founder direction:**

- Parents should be able to supervise children.
- Enterprises should be able to govern employees on company PCs or laptops.

**Working product rule:** Use explicit Supervision Contracts rather than unrestricted surveillance.

### Family edition direction

Possible parent controls:

- tools and models;
- time and spending budgets;
- external contacts and purchases;
- high-risk actions;
- new permissions and sensors;
- age-appropriate Safety Floors;
- serious safety alerts;
- shared family Contexts.

Parent access must not automatically include every conversation, inferred thought, relationship, dream, raw audio/video record, or unrestricted browsing history.

Use age-progressive control and visible child explanations.

### Enterprise edition direction

Enterprise governance may cover:

- company data;
- company accounts;
- company apps;
- company-owned device work Contexts;
- work permissions;
- spending;
- external work communication;
- security, compliance, and company evidence.

It must not automatically include personal phone, home Context, personal health, family, religious, relationship, or non-work activity.

Personal Ghost, Family Context, and Enterprise Context must remain logically and cryptographically separable.

**Still open:** Exact legal, age, jurisdiction, rights, appeal, visibility, and audit specifications.

## FD-07 - Presence

**Founder decision:** Presence is central; personification is optional.

### Mandatory Presence functions

- show or communicate current Context;
- support Come;
- communicate actual capability;
- surface approvals, holds, and stops;
- accept correction;
- support departure and privacy;
- preserve continuity across supported surfaces;
- communicate limits honestly.

### Optional manifestation

- avatar or no avatar
